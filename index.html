How it works

  1. Intake: a form collects the applicant's details and CV.
  2. AI scan: an AI agent reads the application, scores the fit and writes the result to a tracking sheet.
  3. Handler: errors from every workflow are caught and logged in one place.
  4. HR Policy Chatbot: an AI agent answers questions from the company policy documents, and says which policy it used.
  5. Job Application Coach: an AI agent reviews a CV and sends back a coaching report.
