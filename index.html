How it works

Five n8n workflows run the whole system.

  1. IntakeA form collects the applicant's details and CV.
  2. AI scanAn AI agent reads the application, scores the fit and writes the result to a tracking sheet.
  3. HandlerErrors from every workflow are caught and logged in one place.
  4. HR Policy ChatbotAn AI agent answers questions from the company policy documents, and says which policy it used.
  5. Job Application CoachAn AI agent reviews a CV and sends back a coaching report.
